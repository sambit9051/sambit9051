Pressure Analysis - Vertical Walls (Janssen's Theory - IS 4995)

📐 Janssen's Formula for Silo Pressures:
pv(z) = (γR/λμ) × [1 - e-λμz/R]
ph(z) = λ × pv(z)
Where: R = Hydraulic radius (A/U), λ = Lateral pressure ratio, μ = tan(δ), δ = Wall friction angle

Static Condition (Material At Rest)

Parameters: λ = K0 = 1 - sin(φ) ≈ 0.4, δ = φ (full wall friction)

Filling Condition (Dynamic Loading)

Parameters: λ = 0.5-0.6 (increased lateral pressure), δ = 0.7φ - 0.75φ (reduced wall friction due to material flow)

Emptying Condition (Maximum Pressure - CRITICAL for Design)

⚠️ CRITICAL DESIGN CONDITION:
During discharge, flow patterns create maximum horizontal pressures due to:
• Material acceleration towards outlet
• Switch from passive to active pressure state
• Reduced wall friction during flow
• Dynamic amplification effects

Parameters: λ = 1.0-1.2 (active pressure), δ = 0.5φ - 0.6φ (significantly reduced wall friction)
